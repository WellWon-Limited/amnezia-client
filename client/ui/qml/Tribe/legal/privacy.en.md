## 5. Data Retention

Account data (email, subscription status): retained for the duration of the account and 90 days after closure, then permanently deleted. Payment records: held by payment processors as required by applicable law; the Company retains only subscription status.

## 6. Data Storage, Location and Infrastructure

Your personal data is stored on servers located in Hong Kong SAR. The Company does not operate servers in the Russian Federation and does not apply Russian data localisation requirements (Federal Law No. 152-FZ).

All VPN servers operate exclusively on RAM-based (diskless) infrastructure. No user data is written to permanent storage on VPN servers — all server data is automatically wiped upon reboot, providing an additional layer of protection for user privacy.

If you are located in a jurisdiction with data localisation requirements (including Russia), you acknowledge that your data is stored outside your jurisdiction and consent to such cross-border transfer.
